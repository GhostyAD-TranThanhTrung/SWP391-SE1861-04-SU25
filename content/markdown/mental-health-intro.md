# Understanding Mental Health

## What is Mental Health?

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act. It also helps determine how we handle stress, relate to others, and make choices.

## Why Mental Health Matters

Mental health is important at every stage of life, from childhood and adolescence through adulthood. Over the course of your life, if you experience mental health problems, your thinking, mood, and behavior could be affected.

## Common Signs of Good Mental Health

- Ability to cope with life's stresses
- Working productively
- Maintaining fulfilling relationships
- Contributing to your community
- Feeling good about yourself

## Factors That Contribute to Mental Health

### Biological Factors
- Genes
- Brain chemistry
- Family history of mental health problems

### Life Experiences
- Trauma or abuse
- Family history of mental health problems

### Environmental Factors
- Social support
- Living conditions
- Work environment

## When to Seek Help

It's important to seek help when:
- You feel overwhelmed
- You're using substances to cope
- Your relationships are suffering
- Your work or school performance is declining
- You're having thoughts of self-harm

## Getting Started

Taking care of your mental health is just as important as taking care of your physical health. This guide will help you understand the basics and provide you with tools to maintain good mental wellness.

Remember: Mental health conditions are treatable, and recovery is possible.
